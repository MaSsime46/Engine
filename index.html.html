Using it in the car
  1. Mount the phone so it cannot slide. Do not handle it while driving; set it up before you set off, or let a passenger do it.
  2. Connect the phone to the car by Bluetooth and set the car to that input.
  3. Tap Start. The engine follows GPS speed right away. It learns which way is forward from your first gentle acceleration or braking, whether you start parked or already moving.
  4. The line under the speed says whether it comes from GPS or is only estimated. If it says estimated, allow location for this site in iPhone Settings, Privacy & Security, Location Services, Safari Websites.
  5. Keep this page open on screen. Browsers stop the sensors and the sound when the screen locks or you switch apps.
  6. Bluetooth delays the sound by a fraction of a second. That is normal.
  7. If the engine feels too lazy or too eager, change throttle sensitivity. If it revs when you brake, tap Flip direction.
